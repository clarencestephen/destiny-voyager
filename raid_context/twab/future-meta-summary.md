## UPDATE
Monument of Triumph (Destiny 2's final live-service update; patch 9.7.0)

## LAUNCH
2026-06-09 (Tuesday). Source: Bungie "Every End is a New Beginning" (5/21/26) + all three Dev Insights; Aztecross TWAB confirms 6/9.

## WHAT'S CHANGING
- FINAL live-service update for D2 (patch 9.7.0). After this, no active development but game stays playable. Source: BUNGIE 'Every End is a New Beginning' (5/21).
- Massive sandbox overhaul: 3 new aspects (Solar Titan Shieldburst, Solar Hunter Crackshot, Void Warlock Soul Siphon) bringing every non-Prismatic subclass to 4 aspects; new Void Hunter melee Phantom Surge; 2 new Darkness grenades (Stasis Shatter, Strand Slicewire). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB + BUNGIE Abilities & Armor Insight.
- Ward of Dawn fully reworked: 2x bigger 'Thunderdome', pulls/taunts enemies in, weakens enemies + buffs allies, repositionable up to 2x by throwing the core (core is a destructible weak point = PvP counterplay). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB + xHOUNDISHx.
- Armor 3.0 expansion: 6 NEW armor archetypes (Siegebreaker, Skirmisher, Demolitionist, Colossus, Reaver, Powerhouse) on top of original 6. ALL Armor 3.0 exotics retroactively get Tier 5 stats + access to all tuning mods (no re-grind). Ghost archetype mods now bias your chosen archetype to slots 1-2 (was 1-3) = easier targeted farming. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB + BUNGIE.
- Weapon Tier Upgrading: ANY tier-integrated weapon (incl. vault + crafted) can be upgraded toward Gear Tier 5 via a new mod socket for a cost; unlocks enhanced perks/mods/components + T5 cosmetics. Cannot ADD column perks, so god-roll chasing still matters. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Catalyst bonanza: 25 brand-new catalysts on weapons that had none + 9 perks added to old stat-only catalysts. Confirmed: Chaperone=Triple Tap, Parasite=Envious Arsenal, Tarrabah=Ambitious Assassin, Crimson=To the Pain, Merciless=Heal Clip. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Artifacts 2.0: anti-Champion baked into EVERY weapon's intrinsic frame (no artifact column, fires immediately on landing shots). SEVEN artifacts from past seasons available at once + 6 new mods. Disabled in Comp/Trials. Saves in loadouts. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Big philosophy shift: ability uptime ceiling cut and super-from-boss-damage cut to push weapons back into the 50/50 split; offset by across-the-board Primary + Special weapon buffs. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB + BUNGIE.
- Pantheon 2.0 as a permanent activity (new boss roster), SRL returns permanently, Gambit becomes 'Gambit Ops', Director returns to center of UI, Onslaught/Coil/Contest rebalanced, raid+dungeon loot all brought to modern Tier parity with set bonuses. Source: BUNGIE 'Every End'.
- Attune Anywhere: attunement now in your inventory (one weapon per activity type) for much higher targeted drop rates; vendors get Rep Rank reward tracks restored + huge focusing expansion. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.

## BUFFS (stronger)
- PRIMARY WEAPONS buffed across the board in PvE: Legendary primaries +30% vs base combatants, Exotic primaries 30%->40% vs base, non-blaster primaries +15% vs elites, blaster primaries 15%->20% vs elites. Bows additionally +20% base / +40% elite. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- SPECIAL WEAPONS: most get +20% vs elites (excludes snipers, rocket pulses, rocket sidearms, swords, Choir of One). Fusion rifles +7-20% by frame; shotguns +15-20%; slug shotguns +5-10%; snipers +10-15%; trace rifles +20% base/+20% elite (exotic traces 30%->40% base). Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- HEAVY: LFRs +10% vs bosses/champs (Precision +10% scalar, +~30% reserves); heavy GLs & rockets +5% vs boss/champ; crossbows +10% vs boss/champ. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Anarchy buffed: trap lifetime 10s->15s (+50%), trap damage +30%, detonation damage doubled, gains Impromptu Ammunition (replaces Moving Target). Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- The Lament: previous nerf reverted by ~70%, returning final-combo damage + self-heal = strong single-target again. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Vex Mythoclast: intrinsic now stronger Rewind-Rounds-like full-auto; LFR precision kills trigger ignition + full reload on swap; catalyst procs on sustained damage too. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Arbalest +33% vs Champions; Divinity Penance +20% dmg, faster activation (4s->3s), cage in 10 shots (was 14); Heirloom +20% charged impact / +20% vs miniboss+champ / +10% vs boss + Incandescent catalyst. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Jade Rabbit, Touch of Malice, MIDA, Tarrabah, Truth, Wardcliff Coil (max ammo 5->8), Xenophage (+10 reserves), Slayer's Fang, Prometheus Lens, Centrifuse, Hierarchy of Needs all buffed/reworked. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Stasis survivability up: Frost Armor stacks now melt 1-at-a-time (not all at once) = much higher uptime; max-stack DR 31%->35%; all Stasis subclasses make Shatter Shards intrinsically (no Harvest aspect needed); taking damage while Frost Armor grants ability energy. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Many multi-charge / 100%-damage ability buffs: Stormcaller Chain Lightning & Striker Seismic Strike/Shield Bash & Sunbreaker Hammer Strike +100% vs combatants; several melees now 2 charges; Glacial Quake (already top super) gets MORE AoE damage; numerous aspects gain a 3rd fragment slot. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Class-ability-damage aspects now scale with class stat over 100 (Shieldburst, Crackshot, Ascension, Threaded Spectre, Drinker's Lash suspend wave, etc.). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Exotic armor reworks/buffs: Wormhusk (built-in cure loop), Shards of Galanor (Tcrash-tier super dmg), Caliban's Hand, Frostees, Star-Eater-tier Matadoxia (=Cuirass Thundercrash), Skull of Dire Ahamkara (double Nova + Nova Lance combo), Helm of Saint-14 reworked into Ward synergy, Pyrogale, Stoicism/Spirit of Star-Eater for Twilight Arsenal 25%->50%, Pra/Praefectus(+500% melee), No Backup Plans now works on non-Void, Mantle of Battle Harmony, Crown of Tempests, Astrocyte Verse, Dawn Chorus. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Ariana's Vow (Eyasluna) hand cannon damage bonus up to 4.5x. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Older damage perks buffed: Focused Fury 20%->25% (easier to proc), Full Court & Lasting Impression 25%->35%, Reservoir Burst 25%->30%, Adagio on shotguns/bows 20%->30%. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.

## NERFS (weaker)
- ROCKET PULSE RIFLES hit hard: -10% PvE damage, additional -25% vs bosses (stacks to ~-35% on bosses), -25% explosion radius, -15 ammo gen stat, super gen -25% (roamers)/-33% (standard). The 'double special' rocket-pulse-fueling bug is fixed. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- SUPER energy regen from dealing damage to BOSSES reduced by 60% (kills super-spam DPS phases; can no longer farm super mid-DPS). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- ABILITY STAT ceiling cut: max passive cooldown bonus from ability stats reduced 20%; max ability cooldown bonus 190%->125%. Floor unchanged, ceiling lowered to stop ability-spam trivializing combat. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Lucky Pants: bonus damage vs bosses reduced by 33% (pushed out of boss-DPS role); Eya... note: Eriana's/Civil Discourse style outliers tuned — Civil Discourse (Uvalde-style) max bonus reduced to 2.5x. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Outbreak Perfected max Nanite bonus 4.5x->4x (because primaries buffed across board). Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Thunderlord: lightning-strike ammo refund 7->5 (boss-DPS line-up tuning). Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Sunshot & Graviton Lance explosions rebalanced — more damage up close, less at range (less encounter-wiping on one kill). Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Choir of One ammo nerfed (45->35 base, 90->70 enhanced) to follow standard rules; double-special add-clear nerf: special ammo meter gain from minors -17%. Adaptive Burst LFR crate ammo 5->3. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- PvP ability nerfs: multi-charge abilities now START a match with only 1 charge; Knockout no longer grants extra lunge vs players; Golden Gun Deadshot super energy from player kills -40%; Scatter grenade chase distance vs players -50%; Mobius Quiver crossbow buff -50%. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- PvP exotic/grenade safety nerfs: Feedback Fence no longer gives melee DR vs players; Controverse handheld supernova energy return -50% (+ chunk bug fix); Tommy... Dameon (D.A.R.C.I.-fusion) recovery window 0.1s->0.3s; Nothing Manacles scatter seekers 4->3 + no self-chaining; Behemoth Diamond Lance freeze radius reduced vs players. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Grapple melee vs bosses: max grapple-melee bonus from grenade stat -50% and now additive (not multiplicative) with melee stat. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Set-bonus nerfs: Smoke Jumper 2pc 'Ride Together Die Together' DR 50%->40%. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB + xHOUNDISHx.
- Spark/Facet jolt-from-construct (Arc Soul/Onyx Sentry) now has a 4s internal cooldown. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Whisper of Rending... Whisper Rime: max Frost Armor stacks 8->7 (rescaled to keep same max benefit). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Crucible-only: Special/Heavy/Super kill point-gains -33%, Glaive melee gains -20%; Praxic Blade & sword infinite-block ammo economy tightened. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.

## PVE META FORECAST
- Expect a WEAPON-forward DPS meta: with super-from-boss-damage -60% and ability ceiling cut, raid/dungeon damage shifts away from super-spam toward sustained weapon damage. Plan rotations with heavy weapons + DPS exotics, not 6x super yeets. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Primary weapons become genuinely viable in endgame (big across-board PvE buffs) — primaries + the new Anti-Champion-on-every-gun system mean you can run your favorite guns and still stun champs. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- LFRs look strong for boss DPS (Precision +10% scalar, +10% vs boss/champ, +~30% reserves, more ammo per brick). Anarchy returns as a top trap/DPS heavy. The Lament back as top single-target sword. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Rocket Pulse Rifles fall OUT of the boss-DPS meta (heavy nerfs) — pivot away from them for damage phases. Source: BUNGIE Weapons Insight.
- Stasis becomes a top survivability/add-clear pick: 1-at-a-time Frost Armor decay, intrinsic Shatter Shards, 35% DR, new Stasis Shatter grenade (also stuns Unstoppables again). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- Super-damage exotics rebalanced upward (Shards of Galanor, Matadoxia, Star-Eater Twilight Arsenal 50%, Skull of Dire Ahamkara double-Nova) — these become the go-to single-cast burst options now that you can't chain supers. Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.
- New build-battle/experimentation window: 7 artifacts + new aspects + new archetypes = wide-open theorycrafting for the game's final stretch (Aztecross: 'build battles about to go hard'). Source: AZTECROSS TWAB.

## FRAGMENTS
